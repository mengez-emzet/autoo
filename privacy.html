Privacy Policy

How AutoHub collects, uses and protects information when you use this website.

Who we are

AutoHub is an automotive customization and vehicle care business based in Borrowdale, Harare, Zimbabwe. You can reach us by phone on +263 782 406 918 or on WhatsApp at the same number.

Information we collect

We only collect what is needed to respond to your enquiry and to keep the site working:

  • Enquiry details you give us: your name, vehicle details, the services you select and your message. These are sent to us through WhatsApp when you submit the quote form.
  • Enquiry list data: products you add to your enquiry list are stored in your own browser (localStorage) on your device. They are not sent to us until you choose to send them on WhatsApp.
  • Usage information: pages visited, approximate location, device and browser type, collected through Google Analytics as described below.
  • Admin sign-in: if you are an authorised AutoHub admin, Firebase Authentication is used to verify your account. This does not apply to ordinary visitors.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry, prepare a quote and confirm availability, pricing and timelines.
  • To improve the website and understand which pages are useful.
  • To keep the site secure and prevent abuse.

We do not sell your information.

WhatsApp

When you press a WhatsApp link or the quote form button, the message is opened in WhatsApp (owned by Meta) and sent from your own account. WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to that conversation. We only see the message once you send it.

Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand how visitors use the site. Google Analytics sets cookies and similar identifiers. You can read more at Google's privacy policy. You can block analytics by using your browser's cookie controls or an opt-out add-on.

The site also stores a small theme preference (autohubTheme) in your browser so your light or dark choice is remembered. This is not shared with anyone.

Product images added from the dashboard

Product listings shown on the Products page may come from a Firebase Firestore database operated by AutoHub. Public visitors can read these listings but cannot change them. Images uploaded through the admin dashboard are resized in the browser and stored with the listing.

Third-party services

  • Google Analytics (usage measurement)
  • Google Search Console (search performance)
  • Google Fonts (typefaces)
  • Firebase (authentication and product database)
  • WhatsApp / Meta (messaging)

Keeping your information safe

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold, but no website or transmission over the internet is completely secure. Share sensitive details only when you are comfortable doing so.

How long we keep information

Enquiry messages and quotes are kept for as long as needed to serve you and to keep our records. Analytics data is kept according to Google Analytics settings.

Your choices

  • You can ask us to correct or delete the personal information we hold about you.
  • You can clear your browser storage at any time to remove the enquiry list and theme choice from your device.
  • You can use your browser controls to block or delete cookies.

Children

This website is intended for adults arranging vehicle services. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always available on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Call or WhatsApp us on +263 782 406 918. You can also reach the team on the other numbers listed in the footer.
