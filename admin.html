Admin setup needed

The dashboard needs a free Firebase project for the secure sign in and to store the products. Nothing has been connected yet.

  1. Open ADMIN_SETUP.md in the project folder and follow the steps (about 10 minutes).
  2. Paste your Firebase values into firebase-config.js.
  3. Upload the project again and refresh this page.
